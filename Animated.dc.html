Is this content medical advice?

No. Everything on My Diabetes Doc is for educational purposes only. Always talk to your own healthcare team about decisions for your care.

Who is My Diabetes Doc for?

People living with diabetes, their caregivers, clinicians, and anyone looking to improve their metabolic health.

What topics are covered besides diabetes?

Thyroid conditions, osteoporosis, weight loss, PCOS, testosterone deficiency, and adrenal and pituitary disorders.
